Cancel & reschedule patterns
Both flows stay on dashboard — bottom sheet slides up, no navigation
Policy note in cancel sheet — "Free cancellation ends Fri May 9" — pulls from booking policies
Refund note in cancel sheet — only shown if payment.stripePaymentIntentId exists
Cancel confirms with explicit "cannot be undone" text — prevents accidental cancels
Post-cancel: greeting updates to "No upcoming bookings" · cancelled row in past bookings at 50% opacity
Reschedule: current booking shown as amber "Changing" strip — always visible for reference
Current date highlighted amber in calendar · unavailable dates struck through · selected date amber fill
May 17 shown as unavailable — driven by LaneBlock records (venue closed)
Confirm button label includes selected date/time — "Confirm — Wed May 13 at 1:00 PM"
Post-reschedule: green-tinted card + "Rescheduled" badge + "Was: Sat May 10" note
Post-action confirmations are toasts — dark surface, dismissible, auto-hide after 4s in implementation
Toast on light customer surface uses dark (#1C1917) background — high contrast, stands out clearly
Green on light bg uses #065F46 (emerald-900) — passes WCAG AA at all text sizes
Green on dark toast bg uses #6EE7B7 (green-400) — sufficient contrast on dark surface
