Booking policies
These rules apply to all online bookings. Changes take effect immediately for new bookings.
Checkout
Lane hold duration
How long lanes stay held while a customer completes checkout
If checkout isn't completed in time, the hold is released and lanes become available again. 15 minutes is the recommended default.
Minimum booking notice
How far ahead a customer must book online
Prevents last-minute online bookings when staff can't prepare. Walk-in bookings from the cockpit are not affected.
Self-serve changes
Cancellation window
How far in advance customers can cancel online
Outside this window, customers must call to cancel. Shown on the dashboard as "Free cancellation until [date]".
Shown to customers
Reschedule window
How far in advance customers can reschedule online
Can be set independently from cancellation. Large group bookings (19+ bowlers) cannot self-serve reschedule regardless of this setting.
Shown to customers
Group limits
Max online bowlers
Groups larger than this are prompted to call
18
At 18 bowlers, the booking flow shows a "Contact us" gate instead of proceeding. Code-gated packages can unlock bookings above this limit.

Operations
Late check-in grace period
Minutes past start time before a booking is flagged as late
5
After 5 minutes, the booking appears in the Late stat in the cockpit. Staff can then check in late arrivals or cancel to free the lane.
Affects cockpit Late stat
Allow walk-in bookings
Staff can create bookings from the cockpit for customers who arrive in person
Require account to modify
Customers must be signed in to cancel or reschedule online
Prevents abuse of the self-serve cancel flow. Guests without accounts must call to make changes.
